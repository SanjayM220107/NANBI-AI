# 🌸 NANBI — நண்பி

### Your AI-Powered Government Scheme Companion for Women

> **"From not knowing about a scheme to knowing exactly how to apply."**

NANBI (நண்பி) is an AI-powered, multilingual government scheme assistance platform designed for women who may have little or no prior digital experience.

The platform starts with a Tamil Nadu-focused implementation and helps users discover relevant government schemes based on their **language, age, state, and district**.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many women, especially first-time digital users, face difficulties in:

- Discovering government schemes
- Understanding eligibility requirements
- Knowing which documents are required
- Understanding application procedures
- Navigating government websites
- Using digital systems without prior technical knowledge
- Finding reliable information in their preferred language

NANBI addresses this gap by providing a simple, conversational and voice-assisted experience.

---
